1. Subband Spectral Flatness Measure (4–8 kHz):
$$SFM = \frac{\exp\left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N \ln |X(k)|\right)}{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N |X(k)| + \epsilon}$$
• Human Voice: $SFM < 0.25$ (natural vocal tract dampening)
• Neural Vocoders (HiFi-GAN): $SFM > 0.45$ (phase quantization hiss)
2. Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) Variance:
$$\text{Var}(\text{ZCR}) = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{i=1}^K (\text{ZCR}_i - \mu_{\text{ZCR}})^2$$
• $\text{Var}(\text{ZCR}) < 0.008$ indicates absence of biological micro-tremors (synthetic clone).

4. Top 10 High-Frequency Jury Q&A Defense

Q1: "Why use a Vision Transformer (ViT) instead of standard CNNs or ELA?"

Answer: "CNNs rely on local receptive fields that modern generative diffusion models easily mimic. Vision Transformers apply global self-attention across 196 image patches, detecting latent diffusion frequency anomalies and inconsistent boundary lighting that CNNs miss. ELA is kept as a complementary forensic layer specifically for finding copy-paste splicing."

Q2: "What if the circulating photo is 100% genuine, but the story is fake?"

Answer: "That is the exact flaw of single-modality detectors. An authentic 2018 flood photo has real camera noise. Our system combines 64-bit dHash reverse matching against historical disaster archives with RAG verification against live CWC telemetry. The image is flagged as MISLEADING / OUTDATED REUSED MEDIA, not synthetic AI."

Q3: "How do you detect audio deepfakes so fast without cloud APIs?"

Answer: "We run MelodyMachine/Deepfake-audio-detection-V2 (Wav2Vec 2.0) directly on our local CUDA GPU in 24.5 ms. We also compute subband Spectral Flatness (4–8 kHz) to catch vocoder high-frequency hiss and Zero-Crossing Rate variance to detect the absence of natural vocal micro-tremors."

Q4: "How does your system run 100% offline without failing on Windows?"

Answer: "We implemented an in-memory streaming audio loader using Python soundfile, bypassing external ffmpeg dependencies. All model weights (Wav2Vec2, ViT, DistilRoBERTa) and authoritative registries are cached locally."

Q5: "How do you detect coordinated bot behavior across different languages?"

Answer: "We correlate accounts posting within a 180-second window that share the exact same media hash ($d_H \le 4$), but with text in English, Hindi, and Telugu. Even though the text differs, the synchronized timing and identical media hash expose the syndicate."

Q6: "How do you prevent false positives on genuine news?"
